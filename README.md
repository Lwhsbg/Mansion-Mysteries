## Mansion Mysteries
Demo:https://lwhsbg.itch.io/mansion-mysteries
## What it is
It is a horror mansion trope inspired visual novel. You return from a highschool gathering in the way to find an abandoned mansion. Now which path will you take? It is up to you. It has 3 different endings depending upon the places you explore in the mansion.
## Why I made it
I thought of something simple and wanted to make it more vast and bigger as i went on. Although the backstory is quite lacking because of my lack of time, the rooms and the key systems are all on point and i had been thinking of making a game like this for a while.
## How I made it
**Tech stack:** Renpy
