## Mansion Mysteries
Demo:https://lwhsbg.itch.io/mansion-mysteries
## What it is
It is a horror mansion trope inspired visual novel.
## Why I made it
I thought of something simple and wanted to make it more vast and bigger as i went on.
## How I made it
I made it using renpy

I learned many new ways to manipulate renpy codes. I did not have enough time to fully complete the mansions backstory and other qol features due to the lack of time, but this is basically what i wanted to create. I had a hard time with the backgrounds again but i solved the issues very quickly.
